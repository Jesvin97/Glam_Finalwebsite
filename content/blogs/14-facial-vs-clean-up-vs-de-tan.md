# Facial vs Clean-Up vs De-Tan: Which One Do You Need?

**Short answer:** A clean-up is a quick surface clean. A facial goes deeper with massage and a treatment mask. A de-tan targets sun-darkened skin. Pick by your goal and how much time you have.

## Clean-up

A shorter treatment: cleansing, scrub, steam and a light mask. It freshens skin and removes surface dirt.
**Best for:** a quick refresh before an event, or regular upkeep between facials.

## Facial

A longer treatment with cleansing, exfoliation, steam, massage and a mask chosen for your skin type. It works on glow, hydration and deeper cleaning.
**Best for:** dull, tired or dry skin, and regular skin care.

## De-tan

A treatment designed to lighten sun tan on exposed skin such as the face, neck, arms or back. It does not change your natural skin colour.
**Best for:** tan from sun, travel or outdoor work.

## How to choose

- Short on time? Clean-up.
- Want glow and relaxation? Facial.
- Noticeable tan lines? De-tan, sometimes followed by a facial.
- Sensitive or acne-prone skin? Ask for a patch test and a gentle option.

## How often

Many people do a clean-up or facial every three to six weeks, depending on skin type. Do not overdo it.

## Aftercare

Use sunscreen daily, moisturise, and avoid harsh scrubs for a day or two.

**Is a facial better than a clean-up?** They do different jobs. A facial is deeper.

**Will de-tan make me fair?** It removes tan. It does not bleach natural colour.
