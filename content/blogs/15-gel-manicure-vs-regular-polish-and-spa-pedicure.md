# Gel Manicure vs Regular Polish, and What a Spa Pedicure Is

**Short answer:** Gel polish is cured under a lamp and usually stays chip-free for around two to three weeks. Regular polish air-dries and lasts several days to a week. Choose gel for longer wear and regular polish for quick, easy changes.

## Gel manicure

**Pros:** glossy, long-lasting, dries instantly under the lamp, good for events and travel.
**Cons:** must be removed properly, never peeled off, and constant gel with poor removal can weaken nails.

## Regular polish

**Pros:** easy to change, simple removal, wide colour choice.
**Cons:** dries slowly, chips sooner, needs more frequent touch-ups.

## Nail health tips

- Have gel removed by soaking, not peeling.
- Give nails a break between gel sets if they feel thin.
- Moisturise cuticles and hands daily.
- Tell your nail artist about any infection or skin condition first.

## What a spa pedicure includes

A spa pedicure is a longer, more relaxing foot treatment: a warm soak, nail shaping, cuticle care, scrub to smooth rough skin, a moisturising mask or massage and polish. It is good for tired feet and for keeping heels soft in the monsoon.

## Which should you choose?

- Wedding or travel coming up: gel.
- Quick change or seasonal colour: regular polish.
- Rough heels and tired feet: spa pedicure.
