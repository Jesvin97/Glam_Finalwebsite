# Natural Burgundy Hair Colour on Black Hair

**Short answer:** Yes. On black or very dark brown hair, burgundy usually shows as a deep wine tint, most visible in sunlight. A brighter, clearer burgundy needs the hair to be lightened first, which is a bigger step.

## What to expect on dark hair

Black hair carries a lot of natural pigment, so dye alone shows only a little. That is why many people choose burgundy as a soft, natural-looking shade: it reads as dark in the shade and warm red-purple in the sun.

## Two ways to get it

1. **Colour only (subtle):** a burgundy or wine shade applied on dark hair. Gentler, a soft glow of colour.
2. **Lighten, then colour (bolder):** hair is lightened a few levels, then toned to burgundy. The colour is richer but needs more care because lightening dries the hair.

## Choosing the right depth

- Want it to look natural for work or family events? Choose a deep, dark burgundy.
- Want it to show clearly? Ask about highlights or panels with burgundy rather than all-over lightening.

## Keeping it vibrant

Red tones fade the fastest. Wash with cool or lukewarm water, use a colour-safe shampoo, limit heat styling and protect hair from strong sun. Plan a touch-up when the glow dims.

## FAQ

**Will burgundy show on black hair without bleach?** Only as a subtle tint, mostly in light.

**Does burgundy damage hair?** Colour alone is gentler. Lightening first adds dryness.

**How long does it last?** Red fades faster than other shades, so aftercare matters.
