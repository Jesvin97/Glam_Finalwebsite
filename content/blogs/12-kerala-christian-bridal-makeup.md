# Kerala Christian Bridal Makeup: Looks, Tips and Timing

**Short answer:** A Kerala Christian bride usually wants a fresh, elegant look that photographs well in a church and lasts through a warm day. Think soft glowing skin, defined eyes, a natural lip and hair that works with a veil or flowers.

## Planning around the ceremony

- **Church ceremony, then reception.** Keep the morning look soft and classic. A touch-up or change for the reception can make it bolder.
- **Veil.** Plan the hairstyle with your veil or headpiece so it sits securely.
- **Outfit.** Whether you wear a white gown or a cream or white Kerala saree, tell your artist early so base colour and drape match.

## What a good bridal look includes

1. **Skin prep:** a facial a few days before and a calm skincare routine.
2. **Base:** well matched, long-lasting and comfortable in humidity. Ask about HD or airbrush options.
3. **Eyes and lips:** natural tones suit most church ceremonies.
4. **Hair:** a bun, low chignon or soft waves, secured for photos and a long day.
5. **Saree draping or gown fitting:** neat pleats and a comfortable fit.

## Always do a trial

A trial lets you try the look, see how it photographs and fix details. Bring photos, your veil and jewellery.

## Dealing with the heat

Use setting products, blotting papers and keep touch-up items handy. Tell your artist if you perspire a lot.
