# Henna vs Hair Dye for Grey Hair

**Short answer:** Pure henna is a plant-based option that stains grey hair an orange to reddish tone and conditions it. Salon hair dye gives more colour choice and fuller grey coverage, but it is chemical. Neither is simply better. It depends on the look you want and your skin.

## Henna

**Pros:** plant-based, leaves hair feeling coated and shiny, popular and familiar.
**Cons:** on grey hair the result is orange or red rather than black, the colour range is limited, and it is messy and slow to apply.

**Watch out:** some products sold as "black henna" or "instant henna" contain added chemicals, including dye ingredients that can cause allergic reactions. Check the label.

## Hair dye

**Pros:** wide shade range from soft browns to black, better and more even grey coverage, predictable results.
**Cons:** contains chemicals, needs a patch test, and root touch-ups are needed as hair grows.

## Can you use dye after henna?

Often yes, but results can be uneven, and a few combinations react badly. Always tell your stylist if you have used henna, even months ago. A strand test first is the safest route.

## How to decide

- Want a natural-looking black or brown with full coverage? Choose professional hair dye.
- Want a reddish, conditioned look and plant-based care? Henna suits you.
- Sensitive scalp? Ask about a patch test and gentler options before choosing either.

## FAQ

**Does henna cover grey fully?** It stains grey strands but usually in orange to red tones.

**Is hair dye safe for grey hair?** Yes when applied correctly and after a patch test.

**Which lasts longer?** Both need root touch-ups. Colour fades at different speeds depending on shade and care.
