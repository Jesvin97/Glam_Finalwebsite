# Grey Beard and Moustache Dye for Men

**Short answer:** You can colour a grey beard safely with a product made for facial hair, after a patch test. Facial skin is more sensitive than the scalp, so gentle formulas, short timing and careful application matter.

## Why beard colour is different

Beard hair is coarser and often greys faster than scalp hair. The skin on your face is also thinner and more likely to react. A shade that suits your scalp may look too harsh on your beard.

## Choosing a shade

- Pick one or two shades **lighter** than your natural dark. A softer shade looks more natural.
- Keep the beard and moustache matching.
- Consider leaving a little grey for a "salt and pepper" look if you do not want a fully dark beard.

## Before you colour

1. Do a patch test 48 hours before, behind the ear or on the inner arm.
2. Avoid colouring over cuts, razor burn or irritated skin.
3. Trim and shape first so the colour sits evenly.

## How often to redo it

Beard hair grows quickly, so roots show in two to four weeks. Regular light touch-ups look neater than waiting.

## What to avoid

Do not use scalp dye or products meant for other uses on the face. Do not leave colour on longer than directed. Wash off immediately if you feel burning.

## FAQ

**Is beard dye safe?** When used as directed after a patch test, most people tolerate it. Stop if you react.

**Will it stain my skin?** Some colour can mark the skin. A trained groomer applies it carefully.

**Can I do it at home?** You can, but a salon gives a more even result.
