# Itchy Scalp After Hair Dye: Is It an Allergy?

**Short answer:** It can be mild irritation or a true allergy. Itching with redness is common. Swelling of the face, eyes or lips, blisters, oozing or trouble breathing is not normal and needs urgent medical help.

## Irritation vs allergy

**Irritation** often appears during or soon after colouring: stinging, tightness or mild itch that eases once the dye is washed out.

**Allergy** (often to ingredients such as PPD) can build up over hours or a day or two: strong itch, red or swollen scalp, rash on the neck, ears or forehead, and sometimes blisters.

## What to do right away

1. Rinse the dye out thoroughly with lukewarm water and a gentle shampoo.
2. Do not scratch or apply more products to the scalp.
3. Avoid further colouring until the scalp has healed.
4. See a doctor or dermatologist if itching is severe, the scalp swells, blisters or the rash spreads.
5. Go to an emergency service for swelling of the face or throat or breathing difficulty.

## Prevent it next time

- Always do a patch test 48 hours before colouring, even if you have used the same dye before. Allergies can develop at any time.
- Tell your stylist about past reactions so they can choose another approach or advise against colouring.
- Do not colour over a scalp that is already irritated or has cuts.

## FAQ

**Can I be allergic to a dye I have used before?** Yes. Sensitivity can appear later.

**Is itching during colouring normal?** Mild tingling can happen, but strong burning means the dye should be rinsed off.

**How long does a reaction last?** It varies. See a doctor if it persists or worsens.
