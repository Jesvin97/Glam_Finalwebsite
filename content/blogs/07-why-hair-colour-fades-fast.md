# Why Does Your Hair Colour Fade So Fast?

**Short answer:** Colour fades when the dye escapes from the hair. Hot water, strong sun, harsh shampoo, frequent washing and very porous hair are the usual reasons. Red and bright shades fade first.

## Seven common causes

1. **Hot water.** Heat lifts the cuticle and lets colour wash out. Rinse with lukewarm or cool water.
2. **Harsh shampoo.** Strong cleansers strip colour along with oil. Use a gentle, colour-safe shampoo.
3. **Washing every day.** Each wash removes a little pigment. Try every other day if your scalp allows.
4. **Strong sun.** UV bleaches colour, especially in Kerala's sunshine. Cover your hair or use a UV-protecting product.
5. **Heat styling.** Straighteners and dryers fade colour. Lower the heat and use a protectant.
6. **Porous or damaged hair.** Damaged hair cannot hold dye well. Conditioning and repair help.
7. **Hard water, pool or sea water.** Minerals and chlorine can dull and change tone.

## How to make colour last

- Use conditioner or a hair mask regularly.
- Space your washes and keep them cool.
- Book a gloss or toner between colour visits to refresh shine.
- Ask your stylist to choose a shade and formula that suits your hair's condition.

## FAQ

**Is fading normal?** Yes. All colour fades over time. The rate varies.

**Which shades fade fastest?** Reds, coppers and bright fashion shades.

**Does a hair treatment help?** Conditioning and smoothing treatments can improve shine and help hair hold colour.
