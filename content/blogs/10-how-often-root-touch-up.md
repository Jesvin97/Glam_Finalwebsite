# How Often Should You Touch Up Your Roots?

**Short answer:** Most people touch up roots every four to six weeks. Hair grows roughly a centimetre a month, so roots become visible in a few weeks, especially with grey hair and a dark shade.

## What decides your schedule

- **Amount of grey.** More grey means roots show sooner.
- **Contrast.** Black colour over white roots shows fastest.
- **Your tolerance.** Some are happy at six weeks, others want to renew at three or four.
- **Hair growth speed.** It varies from person to person.

## Root touch-up vs full colour

A **root touch-up** colours only the new growth. It is quicker and gentler on lengths.
A **full colour** refreshes the whole head and is needed when lengths have faded. Use it occasionally rather than every visit.

## Stretching the time

- Choose a shade a little lighter than your darkest black. Roots blend better.
- Use a root-cover product only as a short-term fix.
- Ask for a hairline gloss or soft blend to soften the line between colour and roots.

## Don't overdo it

Colouring too often, or pulling colour through the lengths each time, can dry hair. Ask your stylist to apply only to roots unless the ends need it.

## FAQ

**Can I touch up roots at home?** You can, but results can be uneven. Application skill matters.

**How long does a root touch-up take?** It varies. Ask when you book.

**Is a four-week gap too soon?** Not if your grey shows quickly. Just avoid overlapping onto the lengths.
