# Groom Makeup and Grooming Guide

**Short answer:** Groom makeup is light. It evens skin tone, controls shine and looks good in photos without looking made up. Combine it with a fresh haircut, a neat beard and a facial in the week or two before.

## A simple grooming plan

**2-4 weeks before:** Start a basic skincare routine, book your haircut and beard plan.
**1 week before:** Haircut and beard shaping, facial or clean-up, threading if needed.
**2-3 days before:** Light skin care only. Avoid new products or harsh treatments.
**Wedding day:** Shave or trim, then groom makeup.

## What groom makeup usually includes

- A light base to even out tone, never heavy.
- Concealer for dark circles or marks.
- Powder to control shine in warm weather.
- Brows tidied, lips conditioned.

## Hair and beard

Choose a style that suits your face and wedding outfit. A neat beard line and a clean haircut make the biggest difference. If you colour your beard or hair, do it a few days before so it settles.

## Bring this

Photos of looks you like, your outfit details and any skin sensitivities.
