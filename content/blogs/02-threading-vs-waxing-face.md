# Threading vs Waxing: Which Is Better for Your Face?

**Short answer:** Threading is better for shaping eyebrows and small areas because it is precise. Waxing is faster for larger areas such as the upper lip, cheeks or jawline. Neither is "best" for everyone. The right choice depends on your skin and the area.

## Threading

A twisted cotton thread lifts hair out from the root in a line.

**Good for:** eyebrow shaping, upper lip, forehead and fine hair.
**Pros:** very precise, no wax or chemicals on the skin, good for people who react to wax.
**Cons:** can feel sharp on sensitive areas, slower for big areas.

## Waxing

Warm wax is spread over the skin and pulled off with the hair.

**Good for:** larger areas and coarser hair.
**Pros:** quick, removes many hairs at once, skin feels smooth.
**Cons:** can irritate sensitive or thin skin, not suitable over irritated, sunburnt or broken skin.

## Which should you pick?

- **Eyebrows:** threading for a clean, defined shape.
- **Upper lip:** either works. Threading is gentler for sensitive skin.
- **Full face or larger areas:** waxing is quicker.
- **Using retinol, acne treatment or peels?** Tell your therapist first. Your skin may be too sensitive for waxing.

## Aftercare for both

Avoid scrubbing, heavy makeup and direct sun on the area for the rest of the day. Use a soothing, fragrance-free moisturiser.

## FAQ

**Does threading hurt less than waxing?** It depends on the person and the area. Many find brows easier with threading.

**How often should I thread or wax?** Hair regrows at different speeds, commonly every three to five weeks.

**Is waxing safe for sensitive skin?** Sometimes not. Ask your therapist and mention any skin treatments you use.
