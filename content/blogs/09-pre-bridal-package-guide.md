# Pre-Bridal Package Guide: What to Do and When

**Short answer:** A pre-bridal package is a set of skin, hair and body treatments done in the weeks before your wedding, so you look and feel your best on the day. Start about one to three months ahead and never try something brand new in the final week.

## What a pre-bridal plan can include

- **Facials and clean-ups** to clear and brighten skin.
- **De-tan** to even out sun-darkened skin.
- **Hair care:** trims, colour touch-up, smoothing or deep conditioning.
- **Waxing and threading** for smooth skin and neat brows.
- **Manicure and pedicure** for hands and feet.

What is included depends on your skin, hair and schedule. Ask us for a plan that fits you.

## A simple timeline

**2-3 months before:** Skin consultation, start a basic skin routine, plan hair colour or smoothing.
**1 month before:** Facial series, hair colour or treatment, bridal makeup trial.
**1-2 weeks before:** Final facial, waxing, threading, trim. No new products.
**Wedding week:** Manicure and pedicure, rest, water, sleep. Keep it simple.

## Tips

- Patch test any new product or treatment well in advance.
- Tell your artist about sensitive skin or allergies.
- Book your dates early, wedding seasons fill up.

## FAQ

**When should I start pre-bridal care?** One to three months before is typical.

**Is a makeup trial necessary?** It is strongly recommended so you are happy with the look.

**Can I do treatments the day before?** Light ones, yes. Avoid anything new or strong.
