# Keratin Treatment in Humid Kerala: Does It Last?

**Short answer:** Yes, keratin treatment works well against frizz in Kerala's humidity, but results fade gradually. How long it lasts depends on the product, your hair type and how you look after it. Most people see a smoother result for a few months.

## What keratin treatment does

It coats the hair with a smoothing protein-based formula and seals it with heat. Hair looks shinier, feels softer and is easier to manage. It reduces frizz rather than changing your hair type permanently.

## Why humidity matters

Humid air makes hair swell and frizz. A smoothing treatment helps keep the cuticle flat, so hair stays tidier during monsoon and warm months.

## Keratin vs hair smoothening

Both aim for smoother hair. Smoothening and straightening treatments can change the hair's pattern more strongly, while keratin generally focuses on frizz control and shine. Your stylist can recommend the best fit for your texture.

## Aftercare that helps it last

- Follow your stylist's advice on how long to wait before washing and tying hair.
- Use a sulphate-free shampoo and a conditioner.
- Avoid harsh clarifying shampoos and heavy chlorine or sea water.
- Dry and style on lower heat.

## Before you book

Ask what product is used and whether it is suitable for your hair, especially if you have coloured, bleached or damaged hair. Tell the stylist about pregnancy, scalp issues or sensitivity.

## FAQ

**Is keratin good for curly hair?** It can loosen frizz and relax curls somewhat. Discuss the result you want.

**Can I colour after keratin?** Usually after some days. Follow your stylist's timing.

**How long does it last?** It varies by product and care. Expect it to fade slowly.
