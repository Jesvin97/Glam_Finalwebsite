# Is Ammonia-Free Hair Colour Really Safer?

**Short answer:** Ammonia-free colour usually has a milder smell and can feel gentler on the scalp, but it is not automatically allergy-proof or damage-free. It still contains other dye chemicals, so a patch test is still needed.

## What ammonia does in hair colour

Ammonia opens the hair cuticle so the dye can enter. Ammonia-free colours use other ingredients for the same job, so they still change the hair, just often more gently.

## What ammonia-free colour is good for

- Less strong smell during application.
- Often more comfortable for people who dislike the sting or fumes.
- Good for gloss, tone and many grey-coverage jobs.

## What it does not guarantee

- It does not remove allergy risk. Other ingredients, including dye compounds such as PPD, can still cause reactions.
- It may lift or change very dark hair less than conventional colour.
- Some resistant grey hair may need a different formula for full coverage.

## How to choose

Tell your stylist if you have had itching, burning or swelling from any colour before. Ask for a patch test and a strand test, and choose based on your goal rather than the label alone.

## FAQ

**Is ammonia-free colour better for sensitive scalps?** Many people find it more comfortable, but sensitivity depends on all ingredients.

**Does it cover grey hair?** Often yes. A stylist can tell you whether your grey needs a stronger formula.

**Does it last as long?** Longevity depends more on aftercare, shade and hair condition than on ammonia alone.
